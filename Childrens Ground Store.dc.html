About Children's Ground Store

Developing First Nations Language resources and creative cultural products.

Contact Us

Children's Ground Store is an exciting and growing social enterprise that support the creation of unique First Nations Language resources and creative cultural products. All our products are designed, illustrated and written by First Nations children and adults in the communities we work with in the NT. All profits go back to the artists and Children's Ground to support these families and further their enterprise and resource development projects. Our enterprise activities are conducted professionally and comply with the principles of best practice set out in the Australia Council's First Nations Cultural and Intellectual Property in the Arts protocol.
